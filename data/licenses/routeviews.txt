RouteViews at the University of Oregon provides detailed visibility into the global Internet routing system by archiving Border Gateway Protocol (BGP) routing data from collaborative and unique Autonomous System Numbers (ASNs) around the world. RouteViews archives global routing information so network operators, researchers, educators, and policy makers can use the data to better analyze, troubleshoot, and secure the Internet’s core infrastructure.
